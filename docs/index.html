About this scan and what it cannot tell you
  • Article references are review prompts, not compliance findings. A finding means a pattern was present; it does not establish a breach.
  • Articles 9, 12, 14 and 15 apply only to AI systems classified as high-risk under Article 6 and Annex I or Annex III. This scan does not perform that classification.
  • Provider and deployer duties differ. This scan does not determine which role the scanned repository has.
  • Pattern match means the rule looks for a specific pattern in the text. It can still be a harmless test value, so check the context. Needs a human check means the rule uses a heuristic, such as nearby words or file names, and can be wrong in either direction.
  • The readiness score starts at 100 and subtracts points for each finding. It is a rough ranking of the gaps visible in code, not a probability of compliance.
  • It reads files only. It does not run your code, test live behaviour, or review policies, training data or people and process.
  • Not legal advice and not a certified audit.
