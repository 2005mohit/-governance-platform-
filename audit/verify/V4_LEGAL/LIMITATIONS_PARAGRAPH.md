# Limitations paragraph

Use this paragraph verbatim where the legal mapping is reported. It is written to
be read by someone who will not read the tables.

---

This mapping was prepared by the author of the scanning tool, not by counsel, and
it has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Each row records a single provision and a
quotation of fewer than fifteen words, and the quoted text was read in a
third-party reproduction of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 because EUR-Lex returned an
empty response to automated requests at the time of review; the Official Journal
PDF was not read directly. The mapping is therefore **AUTHOR-REVIEWED vs PRIMARY
TEXT, NOT COUNSEL-REVIEWED**, and it is not a legal opinion, a conformity
assessment, or advice that any repository does or does not comply with the EU AI
Act, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the CERT-In Directions.
The strength ratings describe how closely a static pattern corresponds to a legal
obligation; they are not probabilities, and a `strong` rating does not mean the
check is sufficient for compliance. Several rows are `weak` because the detector
sees a proxy (a filename, a configuration value, a nearby log call) rather than
the conduct the provision requires, and a clean scan cannot be read as compliance
in any row. The provider/deployer allocation is simplified to the primary duty
holder in the cited paragraph and does not resolve split duties, Annex III
classification, or the position of a deployer that modifies a system. The India
rows describe proposed rules only; no India rule is implemented in the current
rule pack, the DPDP Rules were not retrieved, and rule 7 on breach notification is
not reviewed or quoted. Nothing in the tables generalises beyond the cited
paragraphs, and nothing in them should be relied on in a regulatory filing,
contract, or dispute without review by qualified counsel.

---

## Short form, for a slide or an executive summary

> Legal mapping is author-reviewed against reproduced primary text and is not
> counsel-reviewed. It is not legal advice, not a conformity assessment, and not
> evidence of compliance. India rows are proposed rules, not implemented checks.

## What would change the status

- Review of every row by a qualified lawyer in the relevant jurisdiction.
- Reading the Official Journal PDF of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 directly, rather
  than a reproduction, and re-checking each quotation against it.
- Retrieval and review of the DPDP Rules, including rule 7, and of any
  notification under s.16(1) of the DPDP Act.
- A decision, recorded in writing, on whether any row should be removed because
  the detector is too far from the provision to be useful.
